### 1. Deadline for commissioning: **20/12/2025**  
*(= 1 day before onboarding of 1st batch of GPs/practices under study KULeuven)*

**Expectation:**  
- ALL GP software packages are connected via SSO to the standalone professional Webapp **PSS Antimicrobial**.  
- An on/off button is available to activate this for doctors participating in the study.

**Procedure:**  
- Complete the attached document by **28/11**.  
- No proof or video is required.

### 2. Deadline for commissioning: **20/03/2026**  
*(= 1 day before onboarding of 2nd batch of GPs/practices under study KULeuven)*

**Expectation:**  
- All GP software packages are connected to the **PSS Antimicrobial** application via **Web Components**, **FHIR API**, or a **combination** of both.  
- An on/off button is available to activate this for doctors participating in the study.

**Procedure:**  
- Fill in the document that will be sent in the week after the trio meeting of **5/12/2025**.  
- This document will include concrete instructions regarding expected deadlines for submitting evidence or scheduling a registration session (likely between **early and mid-February 2026**).

**Additional requirements depending on integration method:**
- If **Web Components** are chosen → **a video must be submitted**.  
- If **FHIR API** is chosen → **a pre-test via the test server** is required **and** an **online test session must be scheduled**.
